Move Schedule into the action row alongside Appearance, with full-width buttons and no selector compression. Keep it an explicit action and preserve unsaved-configuration protection; Save must not open the calendar.

Validation includes regression Red evidence for first-connection gating and matching-platform update state, actual installed browser controls, live connection transition, all downloads, token reveal/copy/failure recovery, quality/error privacy, preview waiting/failure/retry/upload and desktop/mobile screenshots in both themes. Existing Runner, locale and schedule-history owners remain the regression gates.
