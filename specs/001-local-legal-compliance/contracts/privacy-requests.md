# Contract: Privacy Request Route

## Initial channel

The first release publishes a dedicated privacy contact address and a structured explanation of the request process. It does not automatically delete, export, or disclose account data.

## Supported request intentions

- access or knowledge request;
- correction;
- deletion;
- portability where applicable;
- restriction or objection where applicable;
- consent withdrawal;
- complaint or general privacy question;

## Required information

- how to submit without an account;
- what identifying information should and should not be included initially;
- why proportional identity verification may be required;
- that applicability, exceptions, retention obligations, and timelines vary by the request and applicable law;
- how extensions, refusals, or partial fulfillment are communicated;
- applicable supervisory or complaint routes;
- that support chat is not the exclusive privacy-request channel.

## Safety rules

- Do not request passwords, authentication secrets, Twitch tokens, or full payment credentials.
- Do not disclose or delete data until identity and authority are sufficiently verified.
- Record request handling in an access-controlled operational system, not browser consent storage.
- Avoid promising deletion where statutory, fraud-prevention, security, accounting, or dispute records must be retained.
