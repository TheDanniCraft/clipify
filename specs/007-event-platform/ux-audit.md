### Babbler show review — 10 November 2026

The initial shared preview was checked for loading, not for visual readiness. Capturing the real welcome/statistics/media slides exposed a viewport-height error (the notice sat above a full-viewport slide), an empty fourth statistics column, unrestricted description lines and blocked provider frames. The presenter now divides the viewport between notice, stage and footer; statistics fit their actual count and text has a readable width. Category headers stay mounted at the same top position between nominations and results. Winner cards have an accent border and a larger first row, with other nominees below; playback remains manual.

Mobbin references inspected: [Adobe Express slideshow](https://mobbin.com/screens/ccb96f88-909e-49e4-800f-6d4f5c07993e) separates stage from navigation; [Gamma](https://mobbin.com/screens/05649aac-d155-4728-a8ac-c018f058f896) keeps presentation content within a defined canvas; [Circle leaderboard](https://mobbin.com/screens/cd5daccd-6265-486c-be44-b4c182c6c4f0) gives the leading entry a distinct area above the remaining entries. The resulting fifteen hundred ballots produce nonzero percentages without client-side simulated results. Quote entries use text.
