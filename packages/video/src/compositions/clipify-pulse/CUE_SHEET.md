# Clipify Pulse cue sheet

## Delivery

- Channel: primary 16:9 product trailer for the Clipify website, YouTube, and social landscape placements
- Composition: `ClipifyPulse`
- Resolution: 1920×1080
- Frame rate: 30 FPS
- Duration: 735 frames / 24.5 seconds
- Soundtrack: original procedural electronic score generated by `scripts/clipify-pulse/generate-soundtrack.mjs`
- Visual reference: `m2-res_480p.mp4`, supplied by the user, analyzed at its native 30 FPS for pacing, hierarchy, audiovisual cue placement, and energy rather than copied branding or artwork

## Creative direction

- Keep the product visible for most of the runtime. Use current Clipify product captures for playlist building, theme editing, live playback, and controls.
- Match the reference's fast, clean rhythm: oversized uppercase headlines, short feature beats, dark color fields, bright edge light, and decisive camera motion.
- Use Clipify's official color system: `#826aad`, `#6355a0`, `#4a4595`, and `#413f92` for the logo gradient; `#8d42f9` for focused accents; `#100c1b` and `#08060d` for dark surfaces. Reserve pink-red for semantic live indicators only.
- Avoid playful pitched notification runs. The score is driven by drums, bass, texture, and transitions; interface sounds are tactile and sparse.
- Preserve product truth. The captures are current application screens from `public/product`; animation may crop or reframe them but must not invent metrics or user outcomes.
- Follow the reference's changing energy rather than a uniform dance loop: restrained opening, first major hit near 2.53 s, denser product beats around 4–8 s, a steadier middle, strong accents around 14.13–15.20 s, a peak around 16.77–18.10 s, and the end-card resolve near 21.83 s.

## Review notes

- The product screenshots are composition-local copies of current repository assets. Refresh them deliberately when the product UI changes; do not silently point the trailer at mutable application paths.
- Copy is an advertising claim about the intended product experience, not measured retention evidence.
- Continuous music is not restarted at scene boundaries. The table lists the major synchronized anchors; intervening groove transients form the musical bed and do not imply extra UI actions.
- The top spectral edge is a pacing device inspired by the reference's energy; its gradient uses Clipify's exact four logo colors.
