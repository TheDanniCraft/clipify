### Claims and practical limits

- Feature and plan authority: `src/app/components/Pricing/pricing-catalog.ts` plus runtime capability/permission checks. Showing Pro features requires readable Pro identification; “Start free” must not imply the entire shown toolset is permanently free.
- Auto Import is a requested, previewed operation. Do not portray it as a continuously syncing saved playlist.
- Automatic source playback does not require hand-picking a playlist. Avoid “PICK. PLAY.” as the product's defining promise.
- Smart Shuffle is weighted selection, not AI editing or a guarantee that every next clip is the best one.
- Library/backfill coverage can be incomplete. Do not imply all historical clips appear instantly after sign-in.
- Runner uses the creator's own online computer and connection. Show a local device in its broadcast diagram. Advanced scheduling/multiple destinations are Pro.
- Downloads/compositions do not imply auto captions, generative clips, automatic highlight extraction, or direct posting to social networks.
- Analytics describe Creator Page performance. Do not animate fictional viewer gains as proof of break-screen retention.
- Mockups may simplify real controls, split them apart and enlarge them. Their state changes must preserve actual product behavior. Illustrative counts describe the depicted queue or export, not customer outcomes.

## Reference findings and correction

The reference's main sequences are approximately: title assembly (0–2.5 s), match clock and rolling scores (2.5–7.5 s), portrait and counting stats (7.5–10 s), assembling collectible cards and counts (10–13 s), selection/reward interaction (13–17 s), kinetic payoff words (17–18.5 s), accumulating notifications (18.5–21.5 s), brand close (21.5–24.5 s). These are storyboard ranges, not a completed annotation of every source frame.

The transferable pattern is cause → animated state change → readable result. Large isolated components carry each scene; complete app screenshots are unnecessary. Keep one focal action, with a short anticipation, a sharp change and enough result hold to read it.

Earlier reference audio work measured frame-window transients and loudness. That did not establish equivalent musical feel, nor did it constitute a complete frame-by-frame audiovisual annotation. Matching LUFS/LRA was useful for delivery, but insufficient evidence of energy or quality.

## New creative direction

Working line: **YOUR CLIPS. MORE LIFE.**

Promise: turn existing Twitch moments into something people can watch, request, customize, share and broadcast.

One recurring clip card is the visual thread. It travels through the product's uses; each transformation creates the next scene. Use React/SVG/HTML mockups built for motion. Product footage is optional inside the clip; avoid giant static interface captures. Copy stays short, with no voiceover assumed.

Clipify's official logo/purple/dark surfaces frame the campaign. Inside the customization demo, contrast mint/ink with bold sans, orange/cream with a serif, then cyan/black with monospace. Change font, widget placement and progress treatment visibly. These are deterministic creator examples, not new Clipify brand palettes.

Each scene has one dominant transformation. Pro identification appears on the corresponding feature scenes as well as the close. Prefer removing a secondary action over speeding several explanations beyond readability.

The main cut intentionally prioritizes a coherent streamer story. The audit also supports focused follow-ups for library/stream moments, teams/agencies, analytics, AI assistant control, and community/member cards. Those capabilities are accounted for without trying to explain the entire application in 32 seconds.

### Optional longer cut

A 45–48 second website explainer can add a stream-moments timeline before exports, an AI request that visibly configures an approved resource, and team/Creator Page analytics. Keep these as separate beats with readable outcomes. Do not add a rapidly flashing list of every feature to the main cut.

## Music reset

Retire the current Pulse score as the musical foundation. It has slow pad envelopes, soft harmonic material and sparse irregular beats; isolated impact alignment leaves it feeling hesitant. Higher gain cannot repair this arrangement.

Target a modern electronic product promo: provisional 145–155 BPM, tight kick/snare, continuous rhythmic subdivisions, syncopated moving bass, a short memorable synth hook, crisp fills and deliberate brief breaks. Use clear anticipation/release around hero interactions. Avoid long ambient introductions, generic inspirational chord pads, and chains of pitched UI blips.

Build or obtain a strong music bed, then design the visual sequence around its phrases. A high-quality generated track or a commercially cleared existing track is an option, but no dedicated music-generation tool is available in the current session. Procedural synthesis remains possible; judge the actual audition before choosing it as the final bed. The reference's audio is not an asset for the new trailer.

Do not duplicate the reference transient timetable merely to create similarity. Its useful influence is intentional musical phrasing and visible cause/effect. No fixed final BPM, exact score, or generation method is approved by this document alone.

## Production checkpoints

1. **Music + motion audition:** deliver an 8–12 second clip with real rhythmic drive and a queue/playback transformation. Include a music-only listen. Establish subjective energy before building the complete cut.
2. **Three styleframes:** automatic playback/queue, a strongly customized player, and the Desktop → Mobile transformation. Full-size typography and compositing must work before polishing animation.
3. **Rough animatic:** complete 32-second story with mockups and music; confirm readability, product accuracy and feature balance. This is the next full narrative review, not a distribution export.
4. **Detailed sync:** share event constants between visual and sound code; annotate anticipation, contact/state-change, result hold and cue onset in frames. Use exact cue-frame neighborhoods, not only a contact sheet of scene starts.
5. **Finish:** render full, watch with audio, watch muted, listen without picture, inspect boundaries and metadata. Report listening/visual limitations honestly; loudness numbers are delivery checks.
