## Composition-specific notes

- All three aspect ratios share this timeline, copy, soundtrack, and cue table. The social variants restack the OBS mock with the player above chat; the landscape composition retains its original side-by-side layout.
- Format-specific geometry is scoped through `video-root--landscape`, `video-root--vertical`, and `video-root--feed`. Do not alter landscape rules while tuning a social export.
- Viewer losses intentionally use the rhythm `−1, −1, −1, pause, −1, −1, −1`.
- Negative and positive reaction bubbles are illustrative stream activity, not measured product data.
- Positive reactions appear one at a time with deterministic irregular pauses; they never spawn as repeated pairs.
- The rapid 4 → 10 recovery belongs to the bomb reveal and uses lighter notes than the later viewer-gain chimes.
- After the reward clip swap, hype and emoji boxes appear rapidly at scattered positions. Each box keeps its own hold-and-fade timing so the reactions build and clear individually instead of behaving like one synchronized explosion. Feature cards use a clean fade-and-rise entrance so they remain calm and readable.
- Recovery `+1` boxes live in a composition-level overlay aligned to the player. Their fades continue across the Add Clipify → With Clipify scene boundary instead of being destroyed with the outgoing scene.
- Negative and positive reaction boxes use the same composition-level overlay. The final BRB losses and final clip reactions remain above the player while crossing into the next state, then finish their individual fades instead of snapping away at a sequence boundary.
- Final-screen reaction boxes keep moving after their entrance: a restrained mix of breathing, floating, and wiggling prevents the extended hold from feeling frozen.
- The final emoji field is one grouped celebration covered by the achievement chord; it does not add fourteen sound cues.
- The OBS mock's drift is driven by the global visual frame and must remain continuous across scene boundaries.
