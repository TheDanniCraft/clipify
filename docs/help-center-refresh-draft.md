## Replacement Getting Started article

# Get your Twitch clips playing in OBS

Clipify plays your Twitch highlights through a browser source in a BRB or Starting
Soon scene. Your playlists and overlay settings stay saved in your Clipify account.

You need a Twitch account with available clips, an internet connection, and OBS
Studio or another streaming application that supports browser sources. Runner is
optional; you do not need it for an OBS browser source.

### Connect your account

Choose **Start free with Twitch** and connect the Twitch account you want to use.
Eligible new creators receive seven days of Pro without entering card details.
After that access expires, you continue on Free unless you choose a paid plan.
Returning creators do not receive another new-creator trial.

### Choose the clips you want to show

Open your clip library and create a playlist. Preview the clips and save your
selection. Free includes one playlist with up to 50 clips; this is separate from
the unlimited available clip library. Free playback uses Random selection.

If clips are missing, check that they are still available on Twitch and inspect
the dashboard's clip-sync status before rebuilding your setup. Pro playlist
imports run when requested; a saved import is not continuous scheduled syncing.

For clip selection, filters, playback modes and requested imports, see
[Build a Twitch clip playlist](https://clipify.us/guides/twitch-clip-playlists).

### Save an overlay and add it to OBS

Create an overlay, choose its playlist or clip selection, and save it. Copy its
browser-source URL. Keep that URL private; use a Creator Page or gallery link
when sharing clips with viewers.

In OBS, open your BRB scene. Add a **Browser** source, paste the overlay URL, and
set its dimensions to match the scene canvas. Position it with your other scene
elements. You can use a separate scene for Starting Soon.

### Test the scene before going live

Confirm that your selected clips play. Check the OBS audio mixer and browser-source
audio routing. Switch back to gameplay and verify that clip audio does not continue
unexpectedly. The browser source's shutdown, visibility and refresh options affect
what happens when you change scenes; choose and test the behavior you want.

If video does not play, check your saved selection, copied source URL and internet
connection. If only audio is missing, inspect the mixer and browser-source routing.
The homepage demo is silent and does not test your OBS audio settings.

See [Play Twitch clips in OBS](https://clipify.us/guides/twitch-clips-in-obs) for
requirements, limits and troubleshooting.

### Keep control during a break

Pro includes remote playback controls, chat controls, channel points and teammate
access. Configure the intended commands and access restrictions, then test allowed
and denied actions before relying on them live. Give teammates their own access
rather than sharing your Twitch login or private browser-source URL.

See [Control clip playback](https://clipify.us/guides/control-twitch-clip-playback).

### What happens after Pro ends?

Saved resources remain, but Free limits and feature restrictions apply. Paid Pro
continues through its paid-through date when canceled. Runner remains optional,
included with Free and Pro, and runs on your own computer; advanced capabilities
require Pro. It is not hosted streaming infrastructure.

Compare the current offer on [Pricing](https://clipify.us/pricing), or
[sign in](https://clipify.us/login) to build your first scene.

### Suggested title: Plan a Twitch break scene with clips

A clip playlist gives viewers something from your channel to watch while you take
a break. Whether it improves retention depends on your audience and stream; a
working clip player is not evidence of a retention or recommendation-system lift.

Start with highlights that make sense without a long explanation. Review audio,
context and suitability, then save a selection for the kind of stream you plan to
run. Avoid treating every available clip as an approved playlist.

Put the player in a separate BRB scene and test the transition back to gameplay,
including audio. Keep a way to skip an unsuitable clip and give trusted teammates
only the access they need. The linked setup and control guides explain the plan
requirements.

If you want to understand break behavior, record the break's duration, scene,
clip selection and relevant stream context consistently. Compare repeated,
comparable observations and ask viewers for feedback. Concurrent viewers can
change for many reasons; one declining counter does not prove that a particular
scene caused departures. Do not promise a fixed retention percentage, increased
Twitch recommendations or inevitable growth.
